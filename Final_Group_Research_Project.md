1. Assignment overview
For this summative assignment, your group will produce a substantial written project on a social issue
in sport. The purpose of this assignment is to help you examine sport not simply as physical activity or
entertainment, but as a social and cultural phenomenon shaped by wider issues such as inequality,
media, gender, race, nationalism, governance, labour, inclusion, health, or technology.
2. Topic selection
Your group should choose one clearly defined social issue in sport to investigate. You are encouraged
to develop a topic connected to themes discussed during the semester. However, you may also choose
a topic that was not extensively covered in class. In that case, you are strongly advised to discuss your
proposed topic with the course organizer to ensure that it fits the scope of the assignment. When
selecting your topic, please make sure that: the issue is clearly related to sport; the issue is socially and
sociologically meaningful; the topic is specific and manageable; the scope is clearly delimited in terms
of sport, context, case, population, time period, or geographical setting
You may choose to submit your project in one of two formats:
Option A: Academic article / review article
This option is suitable for groups that want to produce a more academic and literature-driven piece of
work. Your paper may take the form of a review article, conceptual discussion, case-based analysis, or
a small-scale academic-style paper.
This format should emphasize: academic literature; theoretical and conceptual discussion; critical
analysis; sociocultural interpretation
Option B: Industry-informed report
This option is suitable for groups that want to write in a more professional or applied style. The report
should still engage with academic ideas, but it should place stronger emphasis on: industry statistics;
policy documents; official reports; organizational data; current sport-sector evidence. This format
should not be merely descriptive. It must still provide critical analysis of the chosen social issue.
Both formats must be grounded in credible evidence and engage seriously with a sociologically
meaningful issue in sport.
3. Core requirements for both formats
Regardless of which format you choose, your project must include: a clear introduction to the topic
and the purpose of the paper; a clear explanation of the sport, issue, and context being examined;
evidence showing why the issue is important; engagement with relevant academic literature; analysis
supported by examples and evidence; a clear conclusion; Your discussion must be based on evidence
rather than personal opinion.
4. Recommended structure
You may adapt the structure depending on your chosen format, but the following structure is
recommended.
-Cover page
Include: course title; title of the assignment; an original and informative title for your project
group name; full names of all group members

*Please list names in order of contribution. If all members contributed equally, state: “All authors
contributed equally to this work.”
-Introduction
The introduction should: introduce the topic; define the social issue being examined; explain why the
topic is relevant; indicate the focus and scope of the project; briefly outline the structure of the paper.
-Literature review / background section (around 1500 words)
This section should review and synthesize relevant scholarship and background evidence.
Requirements: use a minimum of 10 academic references; at least 8 academic sources must be
published in English; prioritize original research articles; focus on scholarship that engages with sport
from a socio-cultural perspective; identify key themes, debates, patterns, or tensions in the literature;
build connections across studies rather than summarizing each source separately; where relevant,
reflect on: theoretical frameworks/research methods/key findings/ limitations or gaps in the literature
-Main analysis section
The title of this section may vary depending on your project. You may organize it using headings such
as: Theoretical Framework/Conceptual Framework/ Historical Background/Case Study Context;
Research Methods; Findings / Results; Discussion
Not every project needs all of these sections, but your structure should be logical and appropriate for
your chosen format.
In this part of the project, you should: explain the context of the chosen sport and issue; analyse the
issue using evidence; provide clearly explained examples; connect the case to wider social, cultural, or
institutional questions; demonstrate critical thinking, not only description
-Conclusion
The conclusion should: summarize the main argument or key interpretations; highlight the main
insights from the project; indicate possible directions for future research or further inquiry; remain
specific and thoughtful rather than overly general
-References
References are not included in the word count; Please follow APA 7th edition carefully; Where
available, DOI numbers should be included in the reference list.

6. Guidance on the use of Generative AI
The use of generative AI tools, such as ChatGPT and similar technologies, is permitted only under the
following conditions:
1. Explicit Permission: Students may use generative AI tools for completing assessment tasks if
explicitly allowed by the instructor for a specific assignment or activity. In such cases, the instructor
will provide detailed guidelines on how these tools can be utilized.
2. Attribution and Transparency: If generative AI tools are used, students must clearly disclose their
usage in their submissions. This includes specifying: The name(s) of the tool(s) used; The purpose for
which the tool was used (e.g., idea generation, drafting text, creating visuals, coding assistance); A
brief explanation of how the output was incorporated into the final work.
3. Academic Integrity: The use of AI tools must comply with the principles of academic integrity.
Misrepresentation of AI-generated content as entirely one's own work, without proper attribution, will
be treated as plagiarism or academic misconduct.
4. Critical Engagement: Students are expected to critically engage with the output of AI tools, ensuring
that their submissions reflect their understanding of the subject matter. Blindly accepting AI-generated

content without review or modification is not acceptable.
5. Restrictions on Unauthorized Use: Unless explicitly permitted, using AI tools to automate the
completion of assessments, bypass academic effort, or create fraudulent content is strictly prohibited
and will result in disciplinary actions.
6. Instructor’s Discretion: The course instructor reserves the right to determine the appropriateness of
AI tool usage for specific tasks. If unsure about whether the use of an AI tool is permissible, students
are required to consult the instructor before proceeding.
*Please note that the course will employ ChatGPT as an auxiliary tool for preliminary review in some
homework/report reviews.
